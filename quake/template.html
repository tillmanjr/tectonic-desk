Data. Earthquake parameters from the USGS ANSS Comprehensive Catalog (ComCat), retrieved once at the time shown above. This prototype does not update. Mechanisms are each event's preferred USGS product (moment tensor where available, otherwise first-motion focal mechanism), drawn as lower-hemisphere equal-area projections with compressional quadrants shaded. Where a solution reports only P and T axes, the double couple is reconstructed from those axes. Significance, felt reports, MMI and PAGER alert are shown only for events whose detail record was read.

Plate boundaries. Bird (2003), An updated digital model of plate boundaries, G³ 4(3), 1027 (PB2002), via the fraxen/tectonicplates repository; segment class and relative velocity from the PB2002 step file. Basemap: Natural Earth via world-atlas and us-atlas.
